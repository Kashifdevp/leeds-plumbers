Common questions

Is this free to use?

Yes. You pay nothing to us. If you hire the plumber, you pay them directly for the work.

Are you a plumbing company?

No. We pass your enquiry to a local plumber. They carry out the work, give you the price and are responsible for it.

How much does a plumber cost in Leeds?

It depends on the job. Many plumbers charge an hourly rate or a fixed price after hearing the problem. Ask for the price before you agree to anything.

Do you pass boiler jobs to Gas Safe engineers?

Yes. Gas and boiler work should only be done by a Gas Safe registered engineer. You can check any engineer on the Gas Safe Register.

What happens to my details?

We share your name, phone number, postcode and job description with a plumber so they can contact you. Read the privacy policy.

Leeds Plumbers Help is an independent referral service. We do not carry out plumbing work. Plumbers who contact you are separate businesses and we may receive a fee from them for referrals.
